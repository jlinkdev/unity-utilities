# Courtyard stone

The three maps combine the broad stone finish from Dimitrios Savva's scan with finer mineral grain from Charlotte Baglioni's scan. Both sources are available under CC0. [Smooth Concrete Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/smooth_concrete_floor) · [Gravel Concrete 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_concrete_03) · [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

The albedo is neutral so each material can tint it. The normal map combines the measured surface slopes at two scales. The roughness map varies the finish across the same aggregate. The shader projects these maps in metres, varies their offset per slab and adds the architectural joints.
