# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Added three seeded styles. Painterly Cavity layers folded dust around an amber cavity, Tidal Veil builds overlapping sculpted banks, and Ember Arc wraps a swept cloud around an open center.
- Added six whole-volume domain warps with signed strength, an independent warp seed, and an optional underlying bend for repeating patterns. The three styles ship with their authored, unwarped compositions at form seed 91 and detail seed 1049.
- Added shared style profiles, local seed overrides, a movable clearing, and opt-in seeded offsets for the two star anchors.
- Added a URP Render Graph renderer with cached density and stellar shadows, opaque depth clipping, and full- or half-resolution rendering.
- Added the Starlit Passage fly-through, sideways camera moves for Tidal Veil and Ember Arc, and grayscale studies for comparing layering, displacement, and fine surface detail.
