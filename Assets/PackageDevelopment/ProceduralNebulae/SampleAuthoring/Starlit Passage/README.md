# Starlit Passage

Open **Ember Arc.unity** in a 3:2 Game view and press Play for an eight-second camera slide along the curved cloud bank. Its Study Camera uses the Starlit Passage renderer override. Reroll **Form seed** to change the sweep, shoulder, hook, and canopy independently. **Reset** restores the profile default of 91.

Open **Shoulder Surface Detail.unity** and press Play to see fine folds on the shoulders. The study uses the same camera move and single star as **Shoulders and Pockets**, with gas emission set to zero.

Open **Shoulders and Pockets.unity** and press Play for an eight-second out-and-back camera slide. The study uses the Starlit Passage renderer override. A square Game view matches the captured study.

Open **Tidal Veil.unity** for the color version of the sculpted banks. Its Study Camera uses the Starlit Passage renderer override. Select a square Game view and press Play to see the lower foreground shelf pass in front of the rear cloud.

Open **Backlit Bank Study.unity** for the grayscale study. The Study Camera uses the same renderer override as the Flight Camera. Use a square Game view to reproduce the reference framing.

Open **Starlit Passage.unity** and press **Play** for the 64-second fly-through. The Flight Camera uses **Starlit Passage Renderer** as its URP renderer override.

Pause or disable **Nebula Flight** to hold a viewpoint. Move **Star** behind a dust lane to see it dim, or move **Violet Companion** to shift the smaller pool of light. Move **Clearing** to open a different route.

Select **Starlit Banks** to explore variations. The default **Painterly Cavity** profile uses form seed 91 and detail seed 1049. Reroll either seed on the volume and use **Reset** to get the profile default back. Leave **Vary Star Positions** off to preserve the two authored lights. Switch **Style** to **Stellar Plumes** to try the same flight through denser lobes. The package also includes **Single Star Plumes**, a prefab with one off-axis star.
